AI-Powered Recipe Customization 🛡️

Transform any recipe from AllRecipes.com by generating prompts for ChatGPT to modify ingredients, cooking methods, or adapt to specific dietary requirements.

Seamless Integration with AllRecipes 🚀

Navigate to any recipe on AllRecipes.com, open the extension, input your desired changes, and click "Generate" to receive a customized version instantly.

User-Friendly Interface 📈

Designed with simplicity in mind, the extension offers an intuitive interface that allows users to easily input modifications and view results without any hassle.

One-Click Copy Functionality 🦉

Quickly copy your customized recipe to your clipboard with a single click, making it easy to save, share, or print your new culinary creation.

Recipe Translation Feature ✨

Break language barriers with the built-in translation feature, enabling users to convert recipes into different languages for broader accessibility.

Scraper History Tracking 🧰

Keep track of your past recipe modifications with the scraper history feature, allowing you to revisit and reuse your favorite customizations.
